## What is Tattoo Machine Power Supply Troubleshooter?

The Tattoo Machine Power Supply Troubleshooter is a free interactive diagnostic wizard that helps tattoo artists identify the root cause of equipment faults. The tool presents a decision tree with seven primary symptom categories: machine not powering on, voltage fluctuations, speed issues, noise problems, connection dropouts, and overheating.

Each symptom leads to targeted follow-up questions about indicator lights, foot pedal status, RCA connections, and machine type (rotary vs coil). Based on user responses, the tool delivers a specific verdict with actionable repair steps. Results are categorized by severity level: "ok" for simple fixes, "check" for component inspection, "service" for professional repair, and "stop" for immediate safety concerns.

The tool covers the full electrical chain from mains power through PSU, foot pedal, RCA cables, machine jack, and internal motor components. It handles both rotary and coil machines, including wireless units.

## How to Use

1. **Select your primary symptom** from six options: machine not powering on, voltage inconsistent, running too fast/slow, excessive noise, connection dropping out, or overheating.

2. **Answer follow-up questions** about indicator lights, foot pedal usage, RCA connections, and machine type. Each answer narrows the diagnosis path.

3. **Review the diagnosis result** which includes a verdict, severity level, and numbered action list. Results show one of four levels: ok (simple fix), check (component inspection), service (professional repair), or stop (immediate safety concern).

4. **Use the back button** to revisit previous questions if you made an incorrect selection.

5. **Click "Start Over"** to run the troubleshooter for a different symptom or machine.

## Use Case Examples

### Case 1: Dead Power Supply During a Session

**Symptom selected:** Machine will not power on

**Question:** Is the power supply unit showing any indicator lights?

**Answer:** No lights at all - completely dead

**Question:** Check the basics - is the power supply plugged in and the outlet live?

**Answer:** Yes - outlet is live, cable is seated

**Result:** Power supply internal fault - verdict: "Unit is receiving no power and showing no signs of life"

**Actions provided:** Try different mains cable, test outlet with another device, check fuse compartment, replace PSU if unresolved.

### Case 2: Intermittent Power Loss During Lining

**Symptom selected:** Cable or connection dropping out

**Question:** Is the dropout happening with all RCA cables or just one specific cable?

**Answer:** Only one cable drops out - others work fine

**Result:** Cable is faulty - verdict: "The machine and PSU are working correctly"

**Actions provided:** Retire the faulty cable, inspect flexing damage near plugs, use new cable, keep spare cable in kit.

### Case 3: Rotary Machine Running Slow After 200+ Hours

**Symptom selected:** Running too fast or too slow

**Question:** Is the machine running faster or slower than expected?

**Answer:** Slower than expected or stalling

**Question:** What type of machine?

**Answer:** Rotary pen or cartridge machine

**Result:** Rotary running slow - likely motor wear or excess load

**Actions provided:** Increase voltage by 0.5V increments to test linear scaling, check needle cartridge binding, inspect motor shaft for debris, consider motor replacement for high-use machines, clean motor contacts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: What equipment faults does this troubleshooter cover?

The tool covers faults in power supply units, foot pedals, RCA cables, machine jacks, motors, capacitors, springs, and bearings for both rotary and coil tattoo machines. It also addresses wireless machine battery issues.

### Q2: Can I use this for wireless tattoo machines?

Yes. The tool includes a specific check for wireless machines in the "running fast" diagnosis path, noting that some units run faster as the battery depletes before cutting off.

### Q3: What do the severity levels mean?

"ok" means a simple connection fix. "check" means inspect a specific component. "service" means professional repair or replacement needed. "stop" means stop using the equipment immediately due to safety risk.

### Q4: How long does the diagnostic take?

Most diagnoses complete in 2-4 questions, taking approximately 30-60 seconds depending on the symptom complexity.

### Q5: Does the tool cover both coil and rotary machines?

Yes. The tool branches specifically for coil vs rotary machines in the speed issue and noise diagnosis paths, providing different actions for each type.

### Q6: What if my symptom isn't listed?

The six primary symptoms cover the most common equipment faults. If your issue doesn't match, start with the closest symptom category and use the back button to explore alternative paths.

### Q7: Can I save or print the diagnosis results?

The tool displays results on screen with a restart option. You can copy the action list manually or use browser print functionality to save the page.

### Q8: Is this tool a replacement for professional servicing?

No. The tool provides diagnostic guidance and recommended actions. Results marked "service" or "stop" indicate professional repair or replacement is necessary.

### Q9: What should I do if the tool says "stop use immediately"?

Follow the safety instructions: disconnect the machine from power, do not attempt to lubricate grinding motors, and contact a professional technician or the manufacturer for service.

### Q10: Does the tool cover power supply calibration issues?

Yes. The voltage fluctuation path checks for unstable readouts, loose knobs, potentiometer wear, and recommends testing at different voltage ranges to identify drift.
